# Gear, Fueling & Checklist

## Before every run
- Shoelaces secure with a double knot; ankle not compressed
- Anti-chafe on inner thighs/groin for longer or wet runs
- Prefer mostly smooth asphalt for key sessions
- Check weather; adjust pace for heat, humidity, wind
- Phone/watch charged; route available

## Fuel & hydration
| Duration | Approach |
|---|---|
| Under 60 min | Normal meals; water by thirst; no gel needed |
| 60–90 min | Small pre-run carbohydrate; water in heat; optional gel practice |
| Over 90 min / trail | Practice 30–45 g carbohydrate per hour |
| Hot weather | Hydrate earlier in the day; don't try to catch up mid-run |

## Core gear
- Road running shoes (primary training + race)
- Longer compression liner / tights (friction control)
- Technical shirt
- Running socks
- Phone holder
- Small bottle / vest for heat and long runs
