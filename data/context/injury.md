# Injury & Recovery Guide

This supports decision-making; it does not diagnose injury. Seek medical care for severe, persistent, focal or worsening pain.

## Traffic-light system
| Status | Looks like | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Awareness only; no altered stride; gone within hours | Continue as planned |
| Yellow | Mild pain, next-day soreness, or worsening on rough surfaces | Reduce intensity/distance; smooth asphalt |
| Red | Sharp pain, limping, focal bone tenderness, pain at rest/walking | Stop running; seek clinical assessment |

## Current patterns
- **Shins:** often noticeable early or on bad surfaces; little/no next-day pain → long warm-up walk; smooth routes; avoid sudden trail jumps
- **Hamstring:** previously irritated by sitting/cycling; now resolved → break up sitting; moderate leg curls; monitor recurrence
- **Knees:** occasional early-run awareness that disappears → warm up; avoid forcing pace on hills
- **Feet/ankles:** fatigue late in longer runs; no persistent pain → secure lacing; rotate shoes when possible
- **Groin chafing:** longer/warm/wet runs → anti-chafe barrier, snug longer liner, change/shower promptly

## Return-to-run checks after a flare
1. Normal walking and stairs
2. No sharp pain during 10 gentle hops
3. No single clearly painful spot on the shin bone
4. Symptoms do not increase during a short easy test run
