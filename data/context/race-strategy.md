# 10K Race-Day Strategy — 27 September 2026

Main risk: starting too fast because you feel good. First 3 km should feel restrained.

## Pacing plan
| Section | Instruction | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Km 1–2 | 10–15 sec/km slower than planned average | Almost too easy |
| Km 3–6 | Settle into realistic race rhythm | Working but controlled |
| Km 7–8 | Hold form and cadence; no panic | Hard but stable |
| Km 9 | Increase only if breathing and legs remain controlled | Commit |
| Final km | Progressively empty the tank | Strong finish |

## Race morning
- Familiar food 2–3 hours before; small banana/toast closer if needed
- Anti-chafe before dressing
- Warm-up: 8–10 min walking/easy jogging + 4 short relaxed strides
- Double knot / runner's knot without over-tightening the ankle
- Do not copy a friend's pace or chase people in the first kilometre
