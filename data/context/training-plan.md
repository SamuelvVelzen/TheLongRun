# 10K Training Plan — Aug–Sep 2026

Race: **27 September 2026**

Weekly rhythm: a few runs plus Thursday bike and optional gym. Days can move week to week — not locked to Tuesday / Friday / Sunday. Warm-up and cooldown walks are additional to listed running distance.

## Pace / effort guide
| Type | RPE | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 3–4/10 | Full sentences; often 6:25–6:45/km, slower in heat |
| Long | 4–5/10 | Calm first half; finish tired but controlled |
| Tempo | 6–7/10 | Comfortably hard; short phrases; not a 5K race |
| 10K effort | 7/10 | Controlled and repeatable; never sprinting |
| Strides | fast-relaxed | 15–20 sec, full recovery, smooth form |

## Adjustment rules
- If Thursday bike + gym leaves heavy legs, Friday quality becomes easy and Tuesday keeps the quality role.
- If shins are painful during walking or tender at one specific spot, replace the next run with easy cycling/walking and reassess.
- If temperature is above 25°C, use effort rather than pace and shorten quality if HR climbs unusually fast.
